# Tiwas - Beyond the Vale of Madness - Multi-LLM Adaptation Reports: System-by-System Comparison

## Status and purpose

**Status**: NON-CANONICAL - advisory only. This report compares, system-by-system, what each of the 25 source LLM adaptation reports said about Tiwas' ability to adapt *Beyond the Vale of Madness — GURPS*. It makes **no rulings**, promotes nothing, and changes no document status. Every classification below carries exactly the authority it had in its source report (Canonical / Locked, Ruled non-canonical, Reserved, Open, Missing, Adventure Content).

**Purpose**: A companion to `tiwas-adapt-vale-of-madness-reports-compilation-2026-09-02.md` (which is organized by **priority tier** and **disagreement register**). This document re-organizes the same 25 reports around **each Tiwas system**, so that for any single subsystem one can see: (1) which LLMs reported on it, (2) what each reported, (3) where the reports agree, and (4) where they disagree. It is intended to help develop Tiwas and to begin the first proper playtest of the system.

## Basis and method

- Compiled from the 25 reports in `investigations/tiwas-adapt-vale-of-madness-reports-1.md` (repaired merged file), with per-report attribution from `investigations/tiwas-adapt-vale-of-madness-reports-1-llm-attribution-2026-09-02.md`.
- **Duplicate correction applied**: the original merge had textually identical Copilot/Copilot 365 duplicate pairs; corrected reports are stored separately and are all treated as distinct viewpoints.
- **Report abbreviations** (LLM - report number): chatgpt-5-2-1 (ChatGPT 5.2), chatgpt-5-5-high-1 (ChatGPT 5.5-high), chatgpt-5-6-1 / chatgpt-5-6-2 (ChatGPT 5.6), copilot-1_1 / copilot-2_1 (Copilot), copilot365-1_1 / copilot365-2_1 (Copilot 365), deepseek-1 (DeepSeek-V3), facebai-laguna-s-2-1-1 (Laguna S 2.1), gemini-1 / gemini-2 (Gemini 3.6 Flash), grok-1 / grok-2 (Grok 4.6), kimi-1 (Kimi K3), metaai-1 / metaai-2 (Muse Spark 1.1), mimo-2-5-1 / mimo-2-5-2 (Mimo 2.5), mistral-1 (Mistral), perplexity-1 / perplexity-2 (Perplexity), recallAI-1 (RecallAI), claude5-1 (Claude 5 Sonnet), glm (glm-4.5-air).
