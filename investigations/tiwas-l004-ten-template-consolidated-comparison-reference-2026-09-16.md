## 0. Governance Notice

This document **unifies** the per-proposal Appendix B tables (which were built
cumulatively — each proposal compared only its predecessors — and, before the Human
column was corrected, partly against the *pre-adoption* Human candidate rather than
the adopted DEC-137 allocation). It:

- re-derives the **Human column from the adopted record only**
  (`investigations/tiwas-human-location-template-l009-l010-adopted-2026-09-13.md`,
  DEC-137, accepted by Tiwa 2026-09-13);

  verified in this pass;
- rules nothing, assigns no DEC, promotes nothing, and changes no source document.
  Where the adopted allocation contradicts a proposal's Human-reference figure, the
  proposal text is **not** edited here — the divergence is recorded in §4 for any
  future use of that proposal.

record, which is adopted designer content (still non-canonical; Canonical entry

## 5. Pattern Summary (observation, not a rule)

- **Tier-1 source classes:** authoritative (Human); reused (Ice Troll, Goblin, Orc,
  Kobold, Hobgoblin, Troll — by assumption, confirmation, interpolation, or
  derivation); bespoke (Blood Man, Dragon, Giant Spider). The de facto boundary —
  stable-limbed biped vs structurally divergent body plan — is the subject of the
  open A/B/C options brief
  `investigations/tiwas-dec100-tier1-reuse-vs-bespoke-designer-options-brief-2026-09-16.md`
  and is **not** settled by this document.
- **Novel mechanisms by instance:** Blood Man — laterality relabel; Dragon —
  structural branch × parity, paired Tier-1 zone, Tier-2 armor exception-carving;
  Kobold — minor-feature sub-node within reuse; Giant Spider — composite/midline
  treatment for genuinely-multiple features, 4-branch scaling; Troll — parent→variant
  derivation; Hobgoblin — sibling interpolation.
- **Open questions remain where they were:** this table does not resolve any source
  proposal's §5/§6 questions (including the four acceptance postures, Goblin/Orc
  proportion calls, Dragon's underbelly width and size tag, and Troll's regeneration
  asymmetry).
