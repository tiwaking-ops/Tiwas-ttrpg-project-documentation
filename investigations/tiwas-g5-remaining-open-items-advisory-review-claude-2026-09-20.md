## 3. Summary of Results

1. **Document defects (6).** Missing or undefined dependencies, undefined shorthand ("Item 4 fixes," "B-item," C1/C2), the omission of DEC-143, and a suggested reordering of the ruling sequence (§4).
2. **Effect on the open list.** If the candidate Z-B (Zone of Control as a reaction category) and D-B (Disengage as a free exit) are chosen, the count of separate consuming mechanics drops from 7–8 to about 5, and U-1, U-2, U-11, Q10 and Q13 become moot (§7).

4. **Recommended ruling order.** Foundation rulings first (Effect/non-Effect classification, Q4 umbrella, Q8, Q9, Q11, U-9), then the ZoC/Disengage cluster, then the movement mechanics, then persistent claims (§8).
5. **Provenance.** The SA-5/E5 divergence is not a mechanics item and should be kept out of the ruling sequence (§9). The review's own advice on it required correction (§10, C-4).
6. **Self-audit.** Nine caveats or corrections to the chat's own earlier output are recorded in §10. **Read §10 alongside §6; several recommendations are narrower than the chat originally presented them.**
