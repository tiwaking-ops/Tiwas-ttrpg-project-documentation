# L-004 Open Questions — Inventory and Blocking-Impact Rank

## 0. Governance Notice

Advisory inventory over the ten DEC-112 L-004 instances (Human v1 adopted, DEC-137;
nine advisory creature proposals). It restates every question each record leaves open
and ranks them by what they block. Ranking is LLM judgment — it confers no authority
and changes no question's open status. Companion records:
`investigations/tiwas-l004-four-creature-template-open-questions-designer-options-brief-2026-09-16.md`
(12-row decision table for the four newest creatures),
`investigations/tiwas-dec100-tier1-reuse-vs-bespoke-designer-options-brief-2026-09-16.md`
(A/B/C on the Tier-1 rule),
`investigations/tiwas-l004-ten-template-consolidated-comparison-reference-2026-09-16.md`
(unified feature table).

**Method.** "Blocks playable content" = prevents a creature from being used in a
playtest session *correctly* (mechanics resolve as designed), as opposed to
framework-consistency work or cosmetic content polish. Tiers:

- **P0** — blocks any creature content being played at all, or corrupts mechanics of
  the creature with active playtest history.
- **P1** — blocks combat-correct use of a specific creature once accepted (armor,
  size, stacking, regeneration).
- **P2** — blocks framework consistency and future authoring, not current play.
- **P3** — proportion/feature polish; templates function as-authored without it.

## 2. Ranked by Blocking Impact

### P0 — play cannot proceed (correctly) without these

1. **ACC — acceptance posture (×9).** No creature template is playable content until
   Tiwa accepts it; everything else is moot for an unaccepted creature. One batch
   decision covers all nine (four-creature brief row ACC + the same call for the six
   earlier instances). *Cheapest unblock in the whole inventory.*
2. **IT-Q3 — reconcile Ice Troll anatomy with its existing stat block.** Ice Troll is
   the only creature with an active playtest series (v3–v6); if the stat block those
   tests used assumes five digits / ears / different size, the template contradicts
   the playtest record. Blocks *continued consistent* playtesting of the family's
   most-used creature.
3. **TR-Q2 — regeneration asymmetry (DEC-088).** Only question touching a ruled
   entry; directly changes combat behavior of both troll variants in non-freezing
   scenes. Recommended path already sketched: Reading A + playtest watch-item.

### P1 — blocks combat-correct use of specific creatures (after acceptance)

4. **Armor cluster (IT-Q2, KB-Q2, TR-Q4, BM-Q4, GS-Q3, DR-Q3-pattern).** Armor Tag
   placement/coverage decides Armor Bypass outcomes; each creature needs its call,
   though each is individually small.
5. **DR-Q2 — Dragon size tag.** No fitting DEC-080 category exists; either rule the
   vocabulary extension or play provisionally at `size_large`.
6. **BM-Q2 — Pseudopod laterality.** Relabel vs suppress changes Wound-stacking
   accumulation — a visible rules difference in play.
7. **OR-Q2 — Orc size class** (derived stats).

### P2 — framework consistency; blocks future authoring, not current play

8. **T1 — DEC-100 vs bespoke** (A/B/C brief exists; codification recommended).
9. **REL — related-creature policy** (derivation vs interpolation).
10. **HREF — stale Human references** in seven proposals (correction appendix task;
    matters when any proposal's comparative argument is relied on).
11. **GS-Q2, DR-Q3** in their convention-forming aspects.

### P3 — polish; templates function as-authored

12. IT-Q4, BM-Q3, GO-Q2/3/4, OR-Q3/4, DR-Q1/4, KB-Q3/4, HG-Q3/4, GS-Q4.

## 3. Reading the Ranking

- The single highest-leverage act available is the **batch acceptance decision (ACC)**:
  it converts nine stored candidates into usable playtest content and costs one ruling.
- The **only register-adjacent item** is TR-Q2; everything else is content-level.
- **Eight of the 36 questions** (counting consolidations) are combat-mechanical (P1
  cluster); **nine are framework** (P2); **the rest are cosmetic** — the family is
  much closer to playable than its question count suggests.
- Nothing in this inventory blocks the *mechanics* themselves: every template resolves
  under the already-ruled DEC-112/136/137/139 architecture; the questions are about
  *which content* fills it.
