# L-004 Ruling Session Agenda — P0 + P1, One Session (~95 min)

## 0. Governance Notice

Advisory agenda sequencing the **P0 and P1 items** from
`investigations/tiwas-l004-open-questions-inventory-blocking-rank-2026-09-16.md`
into one workable session. Each item gives: the **exact decision sentence** to be
approved (Tiwa may edit freely), the evidence, a time estimate, and a recommended
default marked *(rec.)* — LLM advice, no authority. P2/P3 items (T1, REL, HREF, all
cosmetic polish) are deliberately excluded — see §3.

**Ordering logic:** the acceptance gate first (without it the rest is moot for
unaccepted creatures); the two register-adjacent/judgment items next while attention
is fresh; then the small per-creature confirmations, which ride on one framing
decision (the armor pattern).

---

## A1 — Batch acceptance, all nine L-004 creatures — 20 min — **P0**

**Decision needed:** approve the DEC-143 draft's §1 (accept all nine as-authored as
working playtest content, conditions noted) — or strike rows / mark rows
"accepted as edited" / reject. Checkbox options in the draft's §6 sign-off block.

- Source: `investigations/tiwas-dec143-l004-batch-acceptance-ruling-draft-2026-09-16.md`
- Evidence: ten-template consolidated comparison reference; per-row conditions already drafted.
- *(rec.)* **Accept all nine, conditions-binding** — the conditions ARE the operative
  play posture (Dragon provisional `size_large`, Kobold no-Tag, etc.), so signing
  "conditions-binding" leaves the least re-litigating later.
- Scribe prerequisite (before or during session): restore the Ice Troll and Blood Man
  proposals from git history into `investigations/` so register citations resolve.
- **If this fails or needs heavy edits:** jump to A3 (register-adjacent regardless),
  then reschedule A2/A4–A7.

## A2 — IT-Q3: Ice Troll template vs its playtest stat block — 10 min — **P0**

**Decision needed:** *"The L-004 Ice Troll template is the creature's anatomy baseline
for all future playtests; the v3–v6 playtest records stand as pre-template runs with
no contradiction (they resolved Tier-1 coarse zones under DEC-102 and used no size
tag or address tree); the stat block is re-based on the template before the next Ice
Troll playtest."* Approve / amend / reject.

- Evidence (verified 2026-09-16): the BToV-era conversion stat block
  (`investigations/tiwas-gurps-creature-conversion-scratch-ice-troll-blood-man-v0.2-2026-09-03.md`)
  contains **no location tree, no size tag, and no digit/ear anatomical commitments** —
  the template's 4-digit / no-Ears / `size_large` claims therefore face no mechanical
  contradiction in the playtest record. The reconciliation is forward-looking, not a
  retroactive repair.
- *(rec.)* Approve as written — cheapest form: no history rewriting, clean baseline.

## A3 — TR-Q2: Troll/Ice-Troll regeneration asymmetry — 15 min — **P0, register-adjacent**
