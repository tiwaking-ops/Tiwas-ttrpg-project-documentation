## Distinctive content of note (for internal developer awareness only — not rulings)

1. **Different disagreement-register binnings (D1–D7).** This document groups disagreements as:
   - **D1** — whether absence of a literal GURPS-named field (DR/Dodge/Parry/hit-location table)
     suffices to classify a structurally-different-but-Ruled Tiwas mechanism as "Missing
     Subsystem" (Mistral's stricter equivalence standard vs. the majority; Mistral's ~60% Missing
     vs. ~10–40% for the other 24).
   - **D2** — whether fear/mental-stress resolution is Missing Subsystem vs. usable now via a
     Willpower/Resolve Core Test substitution.
   - **D3** — the dispersion of overall readiness figures across reports (Mistral 15%, to Copilot
     365 8/10, with non-comparable denominators), flagged so no single number is treated as
     "the" readiness figure.
   - **D4** — whether damage magnitude for a successful attack/hazard is a distinct gap (Kimi,
     ChatGPT 5.5-high, Claude 5 Sonnet, Mistral, Perplexity, Mimo) vis. already covered by
     Overflow + base-tier Inflict Injury (DeepSeek, Muse Spark, RecallAI, Copilot).
   - **D5** — whether the S-8 named-difficulty-tier system is confidently ready or under-evidenced
     pending an actual populated modifier table (ChatGPT 5.5-high explicit).
   - **D6** — whether missing S-12 creature content is a hard blocker (majority) vs. a bounded,
     already-licensed content task under DEC-076/077 (Copilot, Claude 5 Sonnet, Kimi K3).
   - **D7** — whether a distinct sanity/madness mechanic is a plausible requirement of this
     adventure (raised only by Copilot Report 2, self-flagged as an assumption without adventure
     text; not corroborated by any report with confirmed text access, which treat the operative
     mechanic as the ordinary Fright Check).

   These binnings differ from the D1–D10 register used in the Arena.ai system-comparison; the
   two registers are independent viewpoints over the same evidence and are stored separately.
