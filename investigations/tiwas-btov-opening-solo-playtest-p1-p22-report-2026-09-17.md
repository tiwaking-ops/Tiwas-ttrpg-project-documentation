## 4. Empirical findings (evidence class: empirical finding — no ruling implied)

- **F1 — Overflow, not the monster, is the dominant PC-death mechanism (80% of HP
  loss).** With Starting Value = Cap/2, failed tests pay their natural roll against a
  pool far smaller than typical failing rolls (PE 68; failing rolls 45–95). In this run
  the PC lost 490 HP to overflow vs 121 to the troll's claws. Structural, not
  dice-luck: once PE hit its recovery-pinned floor (Regen 20 → floor(20/2) = +10/test),
  every subsequent test bled `roll − 10` into HP. The adventure's low-skill solo
  premise maximizes exposure to this.
- **F2 — Recovery keeps pools pinned at the floor mid-scene.** floor(Regen/2) applied
  after every test meant the PC's PE sat at 10 for the entire fight and the troll's PE
  never fell below 173. Pool attrition as a pressure mechanism is largely neutralized
  by recovery for anything but overflow spikes.
- **F3 — The DEC-103 Wound channel functioned end-to-end** (Tier-2 attack → shred −1 →
  de-escalation → surviving M3 effects recorded natively), but **every application in
  this fight was ruled-layer, zero canonical** — Canonical §15 reserves conditions,
  wounds, effects entirely.
- **F4 — The defender side of every exchange stood on advisory fiat.** Neither the
  PC's nor the troll's "Active Defense 17"/"Defense 30" has a ruled construction path
  (NPC construction Reserved; the PC's defense was a scenario-authored skill). The
  combat's outcome distribution is therefore sensitive to unruled parameters.
- **F5 — DEC-135's repeated-defense trigger never fired because its parameters were
  never extracted;** the PC defended all 6 exchanges with no fatigue consequence.
  Logged as an open watch-item, not a result.
- **F6 — The adventure's FP economy has no carrier.** The PDF's −1/−2 FP costs were
  converted to advisory direct-PE costs; no canonical or ruled hazard/fatigue mechanic
  was invoked. DEC-079/DEC-135 scaffolds exist but mapping them was out of scope.
- **F7 — Dice-mechanics verification passed:** no 100-Fumble events occurred; the one
  failed Double (99) surfaced the unruled creature-advancement question; all Zero-Step
  transformations and quartile mappings verified by hand against the log.

the BToV material plays, and plays consistently, on non-canonical scaffolding.
