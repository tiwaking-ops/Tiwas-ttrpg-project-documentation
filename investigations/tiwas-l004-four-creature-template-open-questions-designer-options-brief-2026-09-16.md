# DEC-112 L-004 Four-Creature Templates — Open-Question Designer Options Brief

## 0. Governance Notice

This is an **advisory options brief**. It:

- consolidates the sixteen open questions left in the four creature Location Template
  proposals stored 2026-09-13 (Giant Spider, Hobgoblin, Kobold, Troll — all advisory,
  non-canonical, no DEC inferred, per their own §0 flags and the DEC-112 advisory
  storage annotations of 2026-09-16);
- presents option sets with consequences and a **recommended default** per question
  (recommendations are LLM advice only — they confer no authority);
- does **not** rule, assign a DEC, amend the decision register, promote or demote
  anything, or change any Canonical text.

**Tiwa** holds sole human ruling authority. None of the options below is operative
until Tiwa picks one and the ruling is formally recorded.

Source material (all advisory/non-canonical unless noted):
`investigations/tiwas-giant-spider-location-template-proposal-2026-09-13.md`,
`investigations/tiwas-hobgoblin-location-template-proposal-2026-09-13.md`,

## 1. Purpose and Scope

The four proposals are worked instances of **DEC-112 L-004** (per-creature-type
Location Templates). Together with the Human (DEC-137), Ice Troll, Blood Man, Goblin,
Orc, and Dragon instances, they complete a ten-template body of content-authoring
candidates. Each proposal closes with §6 questions awaiting Tiwa. This brief:

- restates each question compactly with its evidence anchor;
- gives the live options;
- recommends a **default** so Tiwa can rule quickly, question by question, or
  wholesale.

Nothing here re-opens settled ground: DEC-136 R2 (no cap) and DEC-139 (full-depth
reading) already rule the terminal-leaf-saturation issue the Ice Troll proposal once
raised, so it is not re-presented anywhere below.

## 3. Per-Question Detail (only where the default needs defending)

### 3.1 ACC — Acceptance posture (all four creatures)

**Evidence.** All four proposals carry the same §0 posture: no register mention, no
DEC-077.A authorization (that covers Ice Troll and Blood Man only), and no DEC-084-
style precedent (which handled Goblin/Dragon specifically). DEC-077's unqualified
default — creature content is Tiwa's own authored content via playtesting — applies.

**Options.** (A) accept as candidates; (B) hold/reject; (C) extend a named
authorization.

**Default rationale.** (A) costs nothing and matches what has already been done for
the other six instances: candidates sit in `investigations/`, usable only when Tiwa
actually authors the creature into content. (C) is available per-creature later and is
best decided when a creature is scheduled for playtest use, not now.

### 3.2 KB-Q2 — No innate armor despite scales

**Evidence.** Kobold §5 recommends no innate Tag, prioritizing the "fragile despite
draconic heritage" trope over scale-implies-armor logic, matching Goblin/Orc. §6 Q2
concedes a narrow Torso-only Tag is a defensible middle ground.

**Default rationale.** Keeping (A) preserves the Goblin/Orc convention and the
DEC-088-era distinction between "wears armor" (equipment) and "has armored hide"
(body Trait). The narrow Tag (B) needs no new work to adopt later — it is fully
specified in the proposal — so nothing is lost by waiting for playtest evidence.

### 3.3 TR-Q2 — The regeneration asymmetry (highest stakes in this set)

**Evidence.** Troll §4: base Troll proposes unconditional Regeneration; DEC-088's Ice
Troll binding gates its Armor/Regeneration/Regrowth package behind `env:freezing`.
Outside freezing scenes Ice Troll is strictly worse than base Troll at the species'
signature trait. Two readings: (A) the combined freezing-gated package (regen **and**
armor) is a net specialization — stronger while frozen, weaker outside; (B) DEC-088
predates any unconditional baseline and may warrant revisiting.

**Default rationale.** Adopt Reading A **provisionally**: the package-trade logic is
coherent, DEC-088 was a deliberate worked example of the Conditional-Trait Binding
grammar, and reopening a ruled entry on a hypothetical (no playtest evidence of the
downgrade actually mattering) inverts the repository's evidence-before-interpretation
posture. Recommended concrete action if adopted: add a playtest watch-item ("does Ice
Troll feel underpowered in non-freezing scenes vs base Troll?") — a low-cost
observation that generates the evidence that would justify (B) if ever needed.

### 3.4 GS-Q3 — Six armor registrations

**Evidence.** Giant Spider §4: full coverage needs six registrations because
registering at the Legs Tier-1 parent would extend coverage into the deliberately-soft
Lower-Leg joints via L-007 parent-covers-child, defeating the called-shot weak points.
§6 Q3 admits the simpler two-registration model ("Legs entirely unarmored,
Abdomen/Cephalothorax armored") as a genuine alternative.

**Default rationale.** The six-registration draft is not complexity for its own sake —
it is what L-007/L-012 produce when a template deliberately mixes armored and
unarmored nodes inside one Tier-1 zone. Simplifying now would resolve a
bookkeeping concern by deleting a mechanical feature (severable joints). If playtest
shows registration bookkeeping is burdensome, (B) remains one edit away.

## 4. Cross-Cutting Items

### 4.1 HG-Q1 + TR-Q3 — related-creature authoring policy (one decision, two proposals)

Hobgoblin demonstrated **sibling interpolation** (a deliberate midpoint of Goblin and
Orc, axis by axis) and left open whether that is ever the right method. Troll demonstrated
**parent→variant derivation** (subtract Ice Troll's §2 ice-specific items) and argues
"related creature" is not one relationship. Options:

- **A. Independent authoring only.** Status quo for seven of ten instances. Every
  template independently justified; interpolation risk (numbers defensible only
  *relative to neighbours*, compounding across generations) avoided entirely.
- **B. Interpolation permitted.** Fastest path for related families; the Hobgoblin
  demo passed every consistency check (100-state sum, laterality, DEC-100 reuse).
- **C. Split policy (recommended default).** Parent→variant **derivation** is
  acceptable where the parent's own text already separates generic from variant
  content (Ice Troll §2 did exactly this); sibling **interpolation** stays
  case-by-case, requiring Tiwa's sign-off per family, because interpolated numbers
  lack independent motivation.

(C) requires no register change today — it is authoring guidance that can live in the
DEC-077 content-authoring workflow whenever Tiwa chooses to write it down.

### 4.2 Framework observations (no ruling requested)

The four proposals collectively complete a three-way authoring pattern the Kobold
proposal names: **reuse-as-is** (Ice Troll, Goblin, Orc, Hobgoblin, Troll),
**reuse-with-added-minor-feature** (Kobold), **bespoke rebuild** (Blood Man, Dragon,
Giant Spider) — decided by whether a new feature needs a top-level zone, and by limb
persistence/structure rather than size or "monstrousness". If Tiwa ever wants this as
standing authoring guidance, it can be captured alongside §4.1 in one pass. No DEC is
proposed here.
