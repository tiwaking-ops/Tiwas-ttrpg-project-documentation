# **Tiwas RPG Project Documentation**

## **1\. Core Philosophy & General Rules**

* **Resolution Mechanic:** d100 Roll-Under. A player must roll equal to or less than their Skill value to succeed.  
* **Rounding:** All fractional math in the game is rounded **DOWN**.  
* **Skill Tiers:** Skills are categorized by Tiers, which represent complexity. A skill's Tier equals the number of base Attributes used to calculate its maximum cap.  
* **Failing Forward:** Failure is the primary driver of character growth. Failing generates Experience Points (XP), and failing with "doubles" (e.g., 22, 55, 88\) unlocks new Advanced Skills.  
* **Exertion Mechanics:** Actions cost resources (Physical Energy or Mental Points) equal to the number rolled on the d100. High rolls are inherently exhausting.

## **3\. Derived Statistics**

Secondary characteristics are derived from summing specific groupings of the 24 core attributes.

| Stat Name | Description | Calculation Formula |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Health Points (HP)** | Physical vitality. Damage or energy overflow reduces this. | Sum of all Body attributes *(bpp, bps, bpe, bpx, bsp, bss, bse, bsx, bep, bes, bee, bex)* |
| **Mental Points (MP)** | Mana/mental capacity used for Mind skills. | Sum of all Mind attributes *(mpp, mps, mpe, mpx, msp, mss, mse, msx, mep, mes, mee, mex)* |
| **Physical Energy** | Pool used to fuel Physical skill rolls. | Sum of *bep \+ bes \+ bee* |
| **Speed** | Determines initiative or tie-breakers in opposed rolls. | Sum of *bsp \+ bss \+ bse* |
| **Energy Regen** | Physical energy recovered. | Sum of *bep \+ bes* |
| **MP Regen** | Mental points recovered. | Sum of *mep \+ mes* |
| **Movement Speed** | Base squares moved in a combat round. | Sum of *(bsp \+ bss) / 15* (Round Down) |

*Note: Characters automatically recover Physical Energy / MP equal to half their respective Regen stat immediately after a test. Full recovery requires a Rest action.*

## **4\. Skills & Action Resolution**

Skills represent a character's actionable abilities.

* **Skill Caps:** A skill's maximum limit is the sum of its underlying attributes divided by its Tier. Skill Caps can be increased by spending General Experience Points (General XP). See Section 5 for more details about increasing Skill Caps.  
* **Base Skill Starting Level:** Half of the maximum skill cap.  
* **Action Cost:** Whenever a skill test is rolled, the player expends Physical Energy (for Body skills) or Mental Points (for Mind skills) equal to the **number rolled on the d100**.  
* **Exhaustion (Overflow):** If a roll's cost exceeds the character's remaining Energy/MP, the overflow is immediately dealt as direct damage to Health Points (HP).

### **Advanced Skills (Dynamic Creation)**

If a player rolls a **Double** (11, 22, 33... 99\) and **fails** their skill check, they have a mechanical epiphany.

1. They immediately create a new Advanced Skill based on the failed action (e.g., failing *Might* to create *Punch* or *Stab*).  
2. The new skill is **1 Tier higher** than the skill tested.  
3. The player selects one additional attribute to add to the new skill's formula. (e.g., A Tier 2 skill uses 2 attributes; a Tier 3 uses 3).  
4. **Starting Value:** The player chooses to start the new skill at Level 1, OR rolls a die up to the new skill's maximum cap.

## **5\. Experience & Progression**

Experience Points (XP) are gained organically through completing adventures, and mechanically through failing skill checks.

**Earning XP through Failure:**

When a skill test fails, the player gains XP equal to: \[Number Rolled\] \- \[Current Skill Value\].

**The XP Pools:**

* **Skill Roll Pool:** This temporary pool holds XP generated from a failed roll. These points are immediately applied to the skill that just failed.  
  * *Naturally Leveling Up:* To increase a skill by 1 point, it costs XP equal to its *current* value.  
  * *Cascading:* If the Skill Roll Pool has enough points to level the skill multiple times, it does so immediately, subtracting the escalating cost each time.  
* **General XP Pool:** Any remainder from the Skill Roll Pool is deposited here. This pool also holds quest rewards. General XP can be spent at any time to upgrade Attributes or Skills (cost \= current value). General XP must cover the entire cost of the upgrade; partial leveling is not allowed. General XP can be spent to increase a skill value above its Maximum Skill Cap (cost \= current value).
